Reviewed decisions

Lewis-dot dimensions by representation

For a structural formula use 4 px dots with a 3 px gap. On a rendered molecular model use 8 px dots with a 5 px gap and 5 px clearance from the atom.

Figure guidelines P63 · Figure guidelines P65 · Master P165

Dashed bond and enclosure units

Use a 1 pt stroke for both. The intramolecular bond has a 6 pt dash; the carbon-chain enclosure has a 4 pt dash and 5 px clearance around the structure.

Figure guidelines P105 · Figure guidelines P107 · Figure guidelines P110 · Figure guidelines P113 · Figure guidelines P115 · Figure guidelines P116

Default equation caption

Use 16 pt CMU Serif Roman for the default caption. The SME-requested variant uses 16 pt Proxima Nova Regular in black. Names and reaction conditions use 14 pt CMU Serif Roman.

Figure guidelines P9 · Figure guidelines P11 · Figure guidelines P14 · Figure guidelines P23 · Figure guidelines P35

Calcium hydroxide sizing

In the Ca(OH)₂ example, use the source inputs H 32, O 64 and Ca 160. With H drawn at 20 px, the drawn diameters are H 20 px, O 40 px and Ca 100 px. The historical chart prints 109 px for Ca.

Master P85

Calculated size in pixels

Use each calculated size as the drawn diameter of the atom. The input atomic radius and the output diameter in pixels describe different quantities.

Master P8 · Master P10 · Master P66 · Master P69 · Master P85

P7
Size of the atom
P8
Irrespective of the final output size, an atom is a simple circle in proportion to its outline thickness, highlight, and shade. In this case, a 20px circle, 2px outline. Decision · Calculated size in pixels
P10
Base atom diameter and outline Decision · Calculated size in pixels
P12
The highlight and shade lie at 70% of the atom’s size.
P14
Highlight and shade position
P16
The width of the highlight and shade is 30% of the atom’s diameter.
P19
Highlight and shade width
P22
In most cases, the highlight always is white and its opacity is 40%. Shade is always 20% darker than the atom’s color.
P25
Highlight opacity and shade brightness
P30
The preferred highlight is from the top-left, hence a 45° rotation of the atoms.
P33
Highlight rotation
P35

P48

P50

Atomic radius for Cl = 100 Step 2 Now Cl = 20px For Ca Multiply 20px with (Atomic radius of the finding atom /atomic radius of the calculated atom) I.e 20*(174/100) =35 Decision · Calculated size in pixels
