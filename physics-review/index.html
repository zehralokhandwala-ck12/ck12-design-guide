Reviewed decisions

Point-charge diameter

Use 25 px for the small circle and 40 px for the large circle. Choose according to available space and detail. The historical prose says 45 px.

T3R2 · T3R3 · P76 · P77

Continuous-charge label sizes

Use 18 px or 22 px. The source lists those two values after referring to three options.

P84 · P86 · P87 · P88

Equipotential stroke weight

Use a 2 pt stroke with a 6 pt dash for the lines and the outline of the surface. The source table gives 2 px; the working rule uses the Illustrator panel units.

T5R1 · T5R2 · P106

Fifth vector color

The previously approved Math and Physics palette places #1590FF fifth. The source table labels this color “senary”.

T2R6

Reading the source figures

P79 illustrates the charge circles but shows outward-pointing arrows around a negative charge. Use P81 for inward-pointing electric field arrows around a negative charge and P110 for outward-pointing arrows around a positive charge. P79 · P81 · P110 · OpenStax electric field lines

P18 demonstrates label placement. For resultant vector color, use the tertiary #7575FC specified in P40 and T2R4. The general vector screenshot shows 3 pt, while the free-body diagram instructions explicitly give 3 px. P18 · P40 · P51 · P61
