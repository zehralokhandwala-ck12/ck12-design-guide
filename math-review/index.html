Math 2025 is the latest consolidated Math guideline and covers most grades. Use the approved rules below when the grade documents differ. Any new discrepancy goes to the design owner for a final decision. The comparison tables transcribe the original documents. Open “Current guide wording” in a topic to see its working specs. “Unspecified” means this comparison has no direct value for that cell. A cross-reference records which document the author named. Math design decisions

The design owner reviewed all 16 items. Each decision records the working rule and links to its source evidence.

Equation text color

Approved · Use #999999 for equation text. Use #666666 when a colored fill makes the default hard to read, including on shaded function and inequality graphs.

Grade 10 T19R4 · Math 2025 T14R8 · Math 2025 T15R7

Angle marker radius and gap

Approved · Start at a 40 px radius with 10 px between arcs, including in Grade 7. Reduce the radius when canvas size or diagram complexity requires it.

Grade 7 P86 · Grade 10 P146 · Math 2025 P160

Number-line point diameter

Approved · Use 10 px in Grades 6–7 and 6 px from Grade 8 onward. This rule applies to points on a number line.

Math 2025 T3R6 · Math 2025 P56 · Math 2025 T10R5 · Grade 7 T3R4 · Grade 9 T5R4 · Grade 10 T14R5

Grade 7 number-line point color

Approved · Use #000000 for Grade 7 number-line points.

Grade 7 T3R4 · Grade 7 T11R4 · Grade 7 P151 · Figure

Excluded · Omitted from the working guide. Original coordinate values are retained in the source passages.

Number-line label size and color

Approved · For a number line within a graph, use 14 px in #999999. For a standalone number line, use 18 px in #999999.

Pie-chart measurement numerals

Approved · Follow Math 2025 for the pie-chart values. Use #000000 for measurement numerals, including percentages and angles placed outside the pie.

Pie-chart palette order

Approved · Use the following order: #80C0FF, #F68DFF, #FFA282, #94E67E, #EAD53B, #A3A0FF, #FF8C96, #7FA2CC.

Sequence diagram grade coverage

Approved · Keep the Grade 6 sequence settings. Grades 7, 8 and 9 use the Grade 10 sequence settings.

Grade 6 T1R2 · Grade 6 P41 · Grade 10 T7R2

Multi-line and multicolor bar graph palettes

Approved · Classify the Grade 10 figure as a multi-line graph. For multiple equation lines, use #EE706B, #5BC9CA, #5EBF43, #A165EF, #2189F9, #F29D38, #CDCA52 in that order. For multicolor bars, use #FF6666, #0DC9BB, #5EBF43, #DA51E5, #3399FF, #ADBD00, #A165EF, #F29D38 in that order.

Grade 10 P293 · Grade 10 P295 · Figure

Grade 6 diagram outlines

Approved · Apply the Grade 7 solid and dotted outline rules to Grade 6 geometry diagrams.

Grade 7 T1R2 · Grade 7 T2R2 · Grade 6 P8 · Grade 6 P41

Equal-length tick marks

Approved · Use matching tick marks for equal-length segments. Standard height is 15 px, with 5 px between ticks. Use 10 px height when the diagram calls for smaller marks.

Grade 10 P173 · Figure · Grade 10 P175 · Figure · Grade 10 P177 · Math 2025 P202 · Figure

Parallel line marker dimensions

Approved · Across all grades, use a 10 px marker dimension. Use 7 px when the image is small and space is tight.

Grade 9 P42 · Figure · Grade 9 P44 · Figure · Math 2025 P214 · Figure

Prime glyph in Illustrator

Approved · Use Latin Modern Roman CK12, 10 Bold Italic, 18 pt. Set the Illustrator Character panel tracking field to 100.

Grade 7 P77 · Grade 10 P98 · Math 2025 P142 · Math 2025 P145 · Figure

Grade 6 construction point diameter

Approved · Use 6 px for construction points in Grades 6 and 7. Ordinary diagram points in these grades use 10 px.

Grade 7 construction points · Math 2025 grade note

Fifth vector palette color

Approved · Use #1590FF as the fifth vector color in both Math and Physics. The source table calls this swatch “senary”.
